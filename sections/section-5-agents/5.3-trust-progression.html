SECTION 5.3

Trust Progression

Earning Autonomy Through Demonstrated Success

The Klarna Playbook

Klarna's 2-year journey: Started with AI suggesting responses that humans sent. After 6 months of near-perfect performance, AI drafted responses humans approved with one click. After 12 months, AI handled routine cases autonomously. Now handles 2/3 of all chats - but high-value cases still get human review.

The Trust Progression Framework

Level Description Human Role Use When
Level 0 AI suggests, human does everything Full control New system, high risk, no track record
Level 1 AI drafts, human approves Review + approve Some track record, medium risk
Level 2 AI acts, human reviews after Spot check Good track record, lower risk
Level 3 AI acts autonomously Exception handling only Proven system, low risk actions

PM Decision Point

You define the promotion and demotion criteria:

  • Promotion threshold: What accuracy % for how long?
  • Demotion trigger: What error rate forces a step back?
  • Emergency brake: What failure type forces Level 0 immediately?

Example: Email Assistant Progression

Week 1-4: Level 0

AI suggests reply topics. Human writes the actual email.

Metrics tracked: Suggestion relevance

Week 5-8: Level 1

AI drafts full emails. Human reviews and clicks "Send" or edits.

Metrics tracked: Edit rate, approval rate

Week 9-16: Level 2

AI sends routine replies. Human reviews daily sample.

Metrics tracked: Customer satisfaction, escalation rate

Month 4+: Level 3

AI handles routine emails autonomously. Complex cases flagged.

Metrics tracked: Resolution rate, CSAT, escalations

Metrics That Drive Trust

Metric What It Measures Target for Promotion
Accuracy % of correct actions >98% for Level 2, >99% for Level 3
Edit Rate % of AI outputs humans modify <5% for Level 2, <2% for Level 3
Rejection Rate % of AI actions humans reject <2% for any level
Escalation Rate % of cases needing human help <10% for Level 3
User Satisfaction End-user feedback on AI interactions >4.5/5 stars

When to Demote

Automatic Demotion Triggers
  • Single catastrophic failure: PII leak, wrong financial action, public embarrassment → Level 0 immediately
  • Error rate spike: 2x normal error rate for 24 hours → Drop one level
  • User complaints spike: 3x normal complaint rate → Drop one level
  • System change: Major model update or new capabilities → Re-evaluate from Level 1
PM Insight

Trust is earned slowly and lost quickly. Klarna took 2 years to reach Level 3. Knight Capital went from trusted to bankrupt in 45 minutes. Build in automatic demotion triggers - they're your safety net.

How to Explain Trust Progression to Stakeholders

"We treat AI like a new employee. Week one, they shadow and learn. Month one, they draft work for review. Month three, they handle routine tasks with spot checks. Eventually, they work independently - but we're always monitoring and can pull them back if needed. This is how Klarna safely scaled to handling millions of customer chats."
