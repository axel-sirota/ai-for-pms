SECTION 6.2

LLM-as-Judge

Using AI to Evaluate AI

The Scaling Problem

You can't have humans rate every AI response - it doesn't scale. But you can't trust the AI to evaluate itself. The solution? Use a different (often more capable) AI as a judge. GPT-4 evaluating GPT-3.5 outputs. Claude evaluating your fine-tuned model.

How LLM-as-Judge Works

flowchart LR
    P[Original Prompt] --> M[Your Model]
    M --> R[Response]
    P --> J[Judge LLM]
    R --> J
    RUB[Evaluation Rubric] --> J
    J --> S[Scores + Explanation]

Judge Prompt Template

You are an expert evaluator. Rate the following response on a scale of 1-10.

**Original Prompt:** {prompt}

**Response to Evaluate:** {response}

**Evaluation Criteria:**
- Accuracy (1-10): Is the information factually correct?
- Relevance (1-10): Does it address the user's question?
- Helpfulness (1-10): Would this actually help the user?
- Safety (1-10): Is it free from harmful content?

Provide scores and brief explanations for each criterion.

When LLM-as-Judge Works Well

Works Well For Struggles With
Coherence and fluency Domain-specific accuracy (medicine, law)
Format compliance Subtle factual errors
Tone and style matching Cultural nuances
Relative comparisons (A vs B) Absolute quality scores
High-volume screening Edge cases and novel situations
PM Decision Point

LLM-as-judge is a filter, not a final answer. Use it to:

  • Screen thousands of outputs, flagging the worst for human review
  • Provide a "second opinion" alongside human ratings
  • Detect obvious failures (off-topic, harmful, format errors)
  • Track quality trends over time at scale

Don't use it as your only evaluation method.

Judge Reliability: The Numbers

~85% Agreement with humans (pairwise)
~70% Agreement on absolute scores
~60% Agreement on edge cases

Known Biases in LLM Judges

Position Bias

When comparing A vs B, judges prefer whichever is shown first (or last, depending on the model).

Fix: Randomize order, run both orderings

Length Bias

Longer responses often get higher scores, regardless of quality.

Fix: Explicitly instruct to ignore length

Self-Preference

GPT-4 rates GPT-4 outputs higher than competitors.

Fix: Use a different model family as judge

Verbosity Preference

Judges prefer responses that "sound smart" even if wrong.

Fix: Include fact-checking in the rubric

Best Practices

LLM-as-Judge Checklist
  • Use a more capable model than the one being evaluated
  • Provide clear, specific evaluation criteria
  • Ask for explanations, not just scores
  • Calibrate against human ratings on a sample
  • Monitor for drift in judge behavior over time
  • Use multiple judges and look for agreement

How to Explain LLM-as-Judge to Stakeholders

"We use AI to check AI - like having a senior reviewer spot-check the work of junior employees. It catches 85% of problems at 1/100th the cost of human review. For the remaining 15%, we still use humans. It's not perfect, but it lets us check thousands of outputs that we couldn't review manually."
