Evaluating AI Features

How Do You Know If Your AI Is Actually Working?

The 3-Day Disaster

Meta Galactica, 2022: Meta launched an LLM trained on scientific papers, claiming it could "write scientific text." Within 3 days, users found it generating fake citations, wrong formulas, and scientifically nonsensical content. Meta shut it down after just 72 hours. The problem? They trusted internal benchmarks that didn't reflect real-world usage.

The Alternative

OpenAI's approach: Before releasing GPT-4, OpenAI ran extensive "red team" evaluations with external experts across domains. They tested for factual accuracy, harmful outputs, and edge cases for 6+ months before public release. The difference? Rigorous evaluation frameworks.
