Embeddings

How AI understands meaning — and why this powers search, RAG, and recommendations.

The Key Idea

Embeddings turn words into coordinates in meaning-space. Words with similar meanings end up close together. Words with different meanings end up far apart.

This is the foundation of semantic search, RAG (Section 4), and recommendation systems.

The Spain-Madrid Example

Imagine plotting words on a 2D map based on meaning:

Spain

[1.0, 1.0]

Country, European, warm

Madrid

[0.9, 0.9]

City, European, warm, capital of Spain

Russia

[-1.0, -1.0]

Country, but very different from Spain

Madrid is close to Spain (0.9 vs 1.0) because they're related in meaning. Russia is far away (-1.0 vs 1.0) because it's a very different context.

Real embeddings use hundreds or thousands of dimensions (not just 2), capturing nuances like tone, topic, formality, and domain.

Similarity Scores: What the Numbers Mean

Score Meaning Example
0.95+ Near-identical meaning "happy" ↔ "joyful"
0.80–0.95 Strongly related "king" ↔ "queen"
0.50–0.80 Somewhat related "king" ↔ "president"
0.20–0.50 Loosely related "king" ↔ "chess"
<0.20 Unrelated "king" ↔ "banana"

Why PMs Care About Embeddings

Semantic Search

Users find what they mean, not just what they type. "How to cancel my subscription" matches "account cancellation policy" even though they share few words.

RAG (Section 4)

Retrieval-Augmented Generation uses embeddings to find relevant documents, then feeds them to an LLM. This is how you ground AI in your company's knowledge.

Recommendations

"Users who liked this product also liked..." works by finding products with similar embeddings — similar in meaning, not just category.

Clustering & Analysis

Group support tickets by topic, cluster customer feedback by theme, find duplicate content — all powered by embedding similarity.

PM Decision Point

When evaluating an embedding model for your product, ask:

  • Domain fit: Was it trained on text similar to yours? (medical, legal, casual)
  • Speed vs quality: Smaller models embed faster but may miss nuance
  • Dimensions: More dimensions = richer representation = more storage cost
  • Cost: OpenAI embeddings cost $0.02/1M tokens. Self-hosted is free but needs GPU.

Stakeholder Framing

When someone asks "Why can't we just use keyword search?":

"Keyword search only finds exact word matches. If a customer types 'my package never came' but our FAQ says 'lost delivery,' keyword search finds nothing. Semantic search using embeddings understands that these mean the same thing. It's the difference between a search that works 40% of the time and one that works 90% of the time."
