1.1 The Stakes

Why 95% of AI projects fail - and what PMs can do about it

Failure Story: Meta's Galactica

In November 2022, Meta launched Galactica, an AI model that could "summarize academic papers, solve math problems, and generate scientific articles." Within three days, researchers discovered it confidently generated fake citations, invented non-existent papers, and produced plausible-sounding but completely fabricated scientific content.

Meta pulled the public demo after just 72 hours. Estimated cost: $10M+ in development, plus incalculable damage to Meta's AI credibility.

PM Decision Point

As the PM for Galactica, you would have needed to decide:

  • What does "good enough" look like before public launch?
  • How do you test for hallucination at scale?
  • What's the rollback plan when things go wrong?
  • Who signs off on a public demo of an AI that can fabricate?

The technology worked exactly as designed. The product decisions around it failed.

Key Insight

The difference between the 5% that succeed and the 95% that fail is not technology - it's PM decisions. The companies that succeed treat AI as a product challenge, not a technology challenge.
